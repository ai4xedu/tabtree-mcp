## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
OKR — Q3
  Objective 1 — Accelerate acquisition
    [ ] KR1 — 40 new customers (vs 25 last quarter)
    [ ] KR2 — Acquisition cost under 300 €
    [ ] KR3 — 3 partnerships signed
    Initiatives
      Redesign the landing page
      Targeted LinkedIn campaign
  Objective 2 — Make delivery reliable
    [ ] KR1 — 95% of milestones met
    [ ] KR2 — Fewer than 5 critical incidents
    Initiatives
      Weekly project review
  Objective 3 — Grow the team
    [ ] KR1 — 2 hires closed
    [ ] KR2 — One training plan per person
```
