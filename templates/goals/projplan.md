## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
📊 Project plan — six weeks
  🎯 Frame @d0..4
    The goal in one sentence — what exists on the last day that does not today
    [ ] Scope written down — and the two things explicitly OUT
    [ ] One owner per workstream, named
    Kickoff @d0
  🔍 Discover @d2..9
    [ ] Talk to the people who live with the problem
    [ ] What already exists — buy, reuse or build
    [ ] Risks listed, each with the day it would surface
  🛠 Build @d8..27
    [ ] The walking skeleton first — thin but end to end
    [ ] The riskiest piece before the comfortable ones
    [ ] Demo every Friday, to someone outside the team
    Mid-point review — cut scope, not quality @d21
  🧪 Prove @d26..37
    [ ] Tested by someone who did not build it
    [ ] The failure path, not only the happy one
    [ ] Numbers agreed at kickoff, measured now
  🚀 Ship @d38..42
    [ ] The rollback path, rehearsed once
    [ ] Who announces, where, with what words
    Ship day @d42
  📦 Close @d43..45
    [ ] The retro — keep, drop, start
    [ ] What we learned, written where the next project will look
```
