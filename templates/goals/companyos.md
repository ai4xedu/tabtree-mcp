## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
🧬 The company on one page
  > The whole business on one screen. Present it to a new hire in five minutes — or to yourself in January.
  Why we exist @a:target
    > The vision is the destination. If a decision moves us closer, it is probably right.
    The world we are building — one sentence
    The enemy: the old way we replace
    In 3 years: true, and measurable
  What we sell @a:gift
    The offer, in the buyer's words
    The promise a customer repeats to a friend
    The price, and why it is fair
  Who buys @a:person
    Our best customer, named
    Where they already hang out
    Who we do NOT serve — on purpose
  How money flows @a:money
    Revenue: what, how often
    The three biggest costs
    The one number we watch weekly
  This year's three bets @a:rocket
    > Three, not ten. A bet you cannot staff is a wish.
    Bet 1 — and the number that proves it
    Bet 2 — and what we say no to
    Bet 3 — and who owns it
  How we work @a:clock
    The weekly rhythm: one meeting, one demo
    What we always write down
    What we never do
  How the world hears us @a:globe
    The channel that actually works today
    One proof: numbers or names
    What we publish every single week
```
