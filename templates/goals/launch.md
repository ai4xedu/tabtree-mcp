## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Launch — product name
  1. Positioning @d0..6
    Who it is for — narrow enough to exclude someone
    The problem, in the words they actually use
    What they use today, and where it disappoints them
    Your promise, in one sentence a stranger can repeat correctly
  2. The offer @d3..9
    What the buyer receives — the deliverable, not the feature list
    Price, and why it is that number and not half of it
    What makes it worth more than what it costs
    Guarantee — and what it costs you if someone abuses it
  3. Proof — what makes a stranger believe you @d5..13
    [ ] Something they can try before paying
    [ ] Three testimonials from people who actually used it
    [ ] One number you can show, not claim
  4. Assets that must exist before you announce @d7..15
    [ ] Landing page — promise, demo above the fold, price, FAQ
    [ ] Buy your own product with a real card, end to end
    [ ] Check the buyer really receives what was sold
    [ ] A support address a human reads
    [ ] Refund path that works without you
  5. Pre-launch — the two weeks before @d14..27
    [ ] Build in public — one post per working day
    [ ] A waiting list with one honest reason to join
    [ ] Five people asked PERSONALLY to post on the day
    [ ] The demo recorded — 30 seconds, understandable with the sound off
  6. Launch day @d28
    [ ] Announce where your audience already is
    [ ] Tell the story, not the feature list
    [ ] Answer every comment for the first six hours
    [ ] One place that collects every question asked
  7. The first thirty days @d28..58
    [ ] Talk to the first ten buyers, one by one
    [ ] Publish what they told you
    [ ] Fix the thing that came up more than twice
    [ ] Second wave — another channel, same story
  8. Decide, on numbers agreed in advance @d58
    Visitors, buyers, conversion — write the targets before launch
    What you do if it works
    What you do if it does not, and by when
```
