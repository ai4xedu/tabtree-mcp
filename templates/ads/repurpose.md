## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
♻️ One idea, ten posts
  > The content engine: structure the idea once, publish it everywhere. This map IS the workflow.
  The core idea @a:idea
    The claim, in one sentence
    Why it matters right now
    The story or number that proves it
  X thread @a:chat
    Hook: the boldest line goes first
    Seven tweets, one idea each
    Last tweet: the ask
  LinkedIn post @a:building
    Open with the pain, not the topic
    One white line after every sentence
    End on a question people can answer
  Short video @a:camera
    > Film it straight from this map — 🎬 Video renders the branch with subtitles.
    First two seconds: the promise
    45 seconds max — cut the intro
    Subtitles always on
  Carousel @a:chart
    Slide 1 the hook, slide 2 the promise
    One idea per slide, ten words max
    Last slide: recap and follow
  Newsletter @a:mail
    Subject line: curiosity beats clarity
    The idea plus one personal story
    PS: the soft sell
  Podcast notes @a:music
    Three talking points from this map
    One disagreement worth exploring
    Clip the best 60 seconds into video
  Next week @a:calendar
    Repost the winner with a new hook
    Turn the best comment into idea #2
    Archive the flops without mercy
```
