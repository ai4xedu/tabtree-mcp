## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Campaign — name and dates
  The one objective
    The number that has to move, and by how much
    The date it is measured
    What we will stop doing if it does not move
  Who it talks to
    The segment, and where they already are
    What they believe today, and what we want them to believe
  The offer
    What they get, and what it costs
    Why now and not next month
    The guarantee or the proof that removes the risk
  Channels
    [ ] Channel 1 — format, frequency, who owns it
    [ ] Channel 2 — format, frequency, who owns it
    [ ] The one channel we are deliberately skipping
  Assets to produce
    [ ] Hook — three versions, not one
    [ ] Visual or video — the format each channel actually needs
    [ ] Landing page — one promise, one action
    [ ] Email sequence — before, during, after
  Budget
    Total, and the split per channel
    The ceiling per acquisition beyond which we stop
  Measurement
    [ ] Tracking in place BEFORE the first euro is spent
    [ ] The dashboard, and who looks at it every morning
    [ ] The debrief date, in the calendar now
```
