## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Content calendar — quarter
  Month 1 · Theme @d0..29
    The one subject everything comes back to
    Campaign running: name and dates
    [ ] Weekly piece — the long one that gets reused
    [ ] Short posts derived from it
    [ ] Newsletter — one send, one idea
    [ ] Launch asset: what has to exist before day 1
  Month 2 · Theme @d30..59
    The subject, chosen because of what month 1 taught us
    Campaign running: name and dates
    [ ] Weekly piece
    [ ] Guest or collaboration — asked a month in advance
    [ ] Newsletter
    [ ] Repurpose the best piece of month 1
  Month 3 · Theme @d60..89
    The subject that sets up next quarter
    Campaign running: name and dates
    [ ] Weekly piece
    [ ] The seasonal moment, prepared three weeks early
    [ ] Newsletter
    [ ] Quarter recap — what worked, published openly
  Always on
    The evergreen piece that keeps bringing people
    The sequence new subscribers receive whatever the month
  Debrief @d85..89
    Which piece brought the most, and why
    Which one we will never make again
```
