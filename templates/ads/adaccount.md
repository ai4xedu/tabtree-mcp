## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Ad account — brand
  🎯 Campaign — Acquisition
    Objective: conversions, one per campaign
    Budget and how it is shared
    Ad set — Cold, broad
      Audience: no interest stacking, let it learn
      Creatives: 3 hooks × 2 formats
      What the ad promises, in one line
    Ad set — Cold, lookalike
      Source: buyers of the last 180 days, 1 %
      Creatives: the proof ones — reviews, numbers
  🔁 Campaign — Retargeting
    Ad set — Visited, did not add to cart
      Window: 7 days
      Message: answer the objection, not the reminder
    Ad set — Added to cart, did not buy
      Window: 3 days, the highest budget of the account
      Message: shipping, returns, guarantee
    Ad set — Bought
      Excluded from every acquisition campaign
      Message: the next product, at the right moment
  🧪 What is being tested right now
    One variable at a time, or the result means nothing
    The date the test ends, decided in advance
  🚫 Exclusions
    Buyers excluded from acquisition
    Employees and existing customers excluded from everything
```
