## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Meeting — topic — date
  Attendees
    Present
    Apologies
  Agenda
    Item 1
    Item 2
  Decisions made
    Decision 1 — approved by whom?
  Action items
    [ ] Action 1 — owner — due date
    [ ] Action 2 — owner — due date
  Open questions
    To settle at the next meeting
  Next meeting
    Date and purpose
```
