## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Register of processing activities
  Data controller
    Entity, address, legal representative
    DPO or contact — name and details
    [ ] Register reviewed this year
  Processing — Customer management
    Purpose — why this data is collected
    Legal basis — contract, consent, legitimate interest or legal obligation
    Data subjects — customers, prospects
    Data categories — identity, contact, billing
    Recipients — internal teams and processors
    Retention period — how long, then what
    Transfers outside the EU — countries and safeguards
    [ ] Security measures documented
    [ ] Record approved
  Processing — Recruitment
    Purpose — select candidates
    Legal basis — legitimate interest
    Data subjects — applicants
    Data categories — CV, history, assessments
    Retention period — 2 years after last contact
    [ ] Security measures documented
    [ ] Record approved
  Processing — Outbound marketing
    Purpose — reach new customers
    Legal basis — consent or legitimate interest
    Data categories — business contact details
    [ ] Opt-out process in place
    [ ] Record approved
  Rights of data subjects
    Response process within one month
    [ ] Privacy notice up to date
    [ ] Request form reachable
  Processors
    List of vendors with access to data
    [ ] Processing agreements signed
  Data breaches
    Notification process within 72 hours
    [ ] Escalation owner named
```
