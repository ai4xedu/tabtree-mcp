## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Training programme
  Audience and prerequisites
    Who this is for
    Expected prerequisites
    Number of participants
  Learning objectives
    By the end, the learner can…
    Second target skill
  Module 1 — module title
    Objective of the module
    Content covered
    Hands-on practice
    Duration
    [ ] Materials ready
  Module 2 — module title
    Objective of the module
    Content covered
    Hands-on practice
    Duration
    [ ] Materials ready
  Module 3 — module title
    Objective of the module
    Hands-on practice
    [ ] Materials ready
  Assessment
    Knowledge check — how it is verified
    Satisfaction survey
    [ ] Assessment rubric written
  Logistics
    Total duration, format, room or remote
    Equipment needed
  After the training
    30-day follow-up
    Resources left with participants
```
