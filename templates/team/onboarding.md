## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Onboarding — role
  Before day one
    [ ] Hardware and access ready
    [ ] Buddy assigned
    [ ] First-week schedule sent
    [ ] Team informed
  First 30 days — understand @d0..29
    Goal: understand the product, the team and the customers
    [ ] Meetings with key people
    [ ] Product training completed
    [ ] First contribution shipped
    [ ] End-of-first-week check-in
  60 days — contribute @d30..59
    Goal: deliver independently on a defined scope
    [ ] Scope of ownership clarified
    [ ] Two deliverables led end to end
    [ ] Feedback on the onboarding itself
  90 days — be autonomous @d60..89
    Goal: own the scope without close supervision
    [ ] Next quarter's goals set
    [ ] Probation review
  Check-in points
    End of week 1 @d7
    Day 30 @d30
    Day 60 @d60
    Day 90 @d90
  Probation success criteria
    What is expected to pass
```
