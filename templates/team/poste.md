## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Role — job title
  Context
    Team and reporting line
    Why this role is open
    Target start date
  Responsibilities
    Primary responsibility
    Secondary responsibility
    What is NOT part of the role
  Required skills
    Must have
    Nice to have
    Ways of working
  Terms
    Contract type, location, remote policy
    Salary range
  Interview guide
    Key skill 1 — question to ask
      What a strong answer sounds like
      Red flag
    Key skill 2 — question to ask
      What a strong answer sounds like
      Red flag
    Motivation and plans
      Why this role, why now
    Candidate's questions
  Process
    [ ] HR interview
    [ ] Technical interview
    [ ] Meet the team
    [ ] Reference checks
    Decision and feedback to the candidate
```
