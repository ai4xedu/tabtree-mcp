## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
System prompt — assistant name
  Role
    Who it is, in one sentence
    The expertise it may claim, and where that stops
  Audience
    Who it answers, and what they already know
    What they will do with the answer
  Task
    The job, as one instruction
    What a good answer contains
    What a good answer leaves out
  Output format
    Shape — prose, list, table, JSON
    Length — a number, not "concise"
    Language and tone
  Constraints
    Facts it must never invent
    When it must say it does not know
    Sources and tools it may use
  Examples
    A realistic input
    The answer you want — the shape, not just the content
    A wrong answer, and why it is wrong
  Edge cases
    Question outside its scope — what it replies
    Missing information — ask, or assume and say so
    Hand back to a human — when, and how
```
