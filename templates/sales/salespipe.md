## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
💼 Sales pipeline
  📥 Deals
    Acme Corp — $12k @proposal
      [ ] Send the revised quote before Friday
    Brightside Agency — $6k @demo
      [ ] Run the demo on THEIR data, not ours
    Nordwind Retail — $18k @qual
      [ ] Budget and timing confirmed by them, not guessed
    Cabinet Lemaire — $4k @lead
      [ ] Find the trigger — why now?
    Studio Karma — $9k @lead
      [ ] One next step with a date, or it is a wish
    [x] Delta Logistics — $15k @won
      [ ] Write down what made it tip — and reuse it
```
