Unit economics — last quarter, by channel
  > LTV = ARPA × margin ÷ churn. Payback = CAC ÷ monthly gross profit. The rule of thumb everyone quotes is LTV/CAC above 3 and payback under 12 months — but read the two together: a beautiful ratio with a 14-month payback still eats the cash you do not have.

    Customers: 40

    Margin: 88%
    Churn: 1.8%

    > Slow to start, and the only line that keeps paying after you stop spending.
    Spend: 6000
    Customers: 95
    ARPA: 29
    Margin: 90%
    Churn: 6%
  📣 LinkedIn Ads
    > Expensive per customer, but they stay — bigger accounts, lower churn.
    Spend: 9000
    Customers: 18
    ARPA: 149
    Margin: 85%
    Churn: 2.5%
  🔍 Google Ads
    > The predictable one. Watch the payback, not the ratio.
    Spend: 12000
    Customers: 60
    ARPA: 49
    Margin: 82%
    Churn: 4%
  🎤 Events & conferences
