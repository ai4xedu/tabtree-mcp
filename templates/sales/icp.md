## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Ideal customer — segment
  Who exactly
    Company size, sector, and the moment they start needing this
    Their job title, and who signs above them
    One segment I deliberately do NOT serve
  The trigger
    What happened last week that made this urgent
    What they tried first, and why it did not hold
    What it costs them to change nothing
  Their words, not mine
    [ ] Five sentences copied verbatim from a call or a review
    [ ] The word they use for the problem — use that word everywhere
  Objections, and the honest answer
    "Too expensive" — compare with what, exactly?
    "We already have something" — what does theirs not do?
    "Not the right time" — what would make it the right time?
    "I need to ask someone" — who, and what do they need to hear?
    "I do not trust it yet" — what proof would settle it?
  Where they already are
    The three places they read, watch or ask
    Who they already trust on this subject
  Proof
    [ ] Talked to five of them this quarter
    [ ] One sentence they said that I could not have invented
```
