## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
AI at — team or company
  Inventory — candidate tasks
    Task 1 — repetitive and text-heavy?
    Task 2 — repetitive and text-heavy?
    Task 3 — repetitive and text-heavy?
  Score each task
    Value — hours saved per week
    Risk if the AI is wrong — who checks?
    Data — is it allowed to leave the building?
  Pilots — pick two, not ten
    Pilot 1
      [ ] Owner named
      [ ] Success metric agreed before starting
      [ ] Two-week review booked
    Pilot 2
      [ ] Owner named
      [ ] Success metric agreed before starting
  Rules of the game
    [ ] What may never be pasted into an AI
    [ ] Who validates AI output before it ships
  Training
    [ ] Everyone has written 10 real prompts
    [ ] A shared prompt library exists
```
