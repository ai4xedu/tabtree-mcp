## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Process — name
  How it works today
    Step 1 — who does it, how long
    Step 2 — who does it, how long
    Step 3 — who does it, how long
    Where it stalls, and why
    Time and cost per run
  Where AI actually helps
    A step that is text-heavy and repetitive
    A step where a draft is enough — a human finishes it
    Steps to leave alone — judgement, relationships, liability
  The redesigned flow
    New step 1 — human or AI, and what it produces
    New step 2 — human or AI, and what it produces
    [ ] Checkpoint where a human must approve
    What we stop doing entirely
  Prove it small
    [ ] Ten real runs, old way and new way
    Time saved per run — the number
    Errors introduced — counted, not assumed
  Make it stick
    [ ] Written procedure updated
    [ ] Owner of the new flow named
    [ ] Team trained on the new steps
  Watch
    The metric reviewed monthly
    The signal that would make us roll it back
```
