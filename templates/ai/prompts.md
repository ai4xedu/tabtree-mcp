## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Prompt library
  Writing
    Rewrite in my voice — paste 3 samples, then the draft
    Summary for a decision — audience, stake, 10 lines max
    Difficult email — the facts, the ask, the tone you want
  Analysis
    Compare options — criteria first, then a table
    Find the flaw — ask it to argue AGAINST the plan
    Explain this to me — as to a smart intern
  Meetings
    Transcript to minutes — decisions, actions, owners
    Prepare the tough questions for this agenda
  Rules that improve any prompt
    Give the role, the audience and the format
    Show one example of the output you want
    Ask it to ask questions before answering
```
