## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
AI usage policy — organisation
  Scope
    Who it applies to — staff, contractors, interns
    Which tools are covered
    Version and date
  Approved tools
    Tool 1 — approved for what
    Tool 2 — approved for what
    [ ] Personal accounts — allowed or not, decided
  Data rules
    [ ] Never paste — customer personal data, credentials, contracts
    [ ] What may be pasted, in plain words
    Where the data goes, and whether it trains a model
  Accountability
    Whoever publishes AI output owns it
    [ ] Output touching money, law or safety is reviewed by a named human
  Disclosure
    When we tell a client that AI was involved
    [ ] Rule for candidates, and for public content
  Skills
    [ ] Everyone trained once, with real examples
    [ ] A shared prompt library exists
  Incidents
    [ ] Where to report a bad AI outcome
    Who can suspend a tool
  Review
    [ ] Reviewed every six months
    [ ] Read by legal or the DPO
```
