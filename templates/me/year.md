## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
My year — 2026
  Wins
    The one I did not expect
    The one that took longest
    The one nobody noticed
  Numbers
    What I shipped
    What it earned
    What I can do now that I could not
  People
    Who changed my year
    Who I helped
    Who I want to work with next
  Lessons
    What I would do again tomorrow
    What I will stop doing
    What I am still avoiding
  Costs
    What it took out of me
    What I gave up to get here
  Next year
    The one thing that matters
    What I say no to
    How I will know it worked
```
