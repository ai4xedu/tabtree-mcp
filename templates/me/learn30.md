## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
🎓 Learn anything — 30 days
  > Pick one skill. One month. This map is the contract with yourself — tick the boxes as you go.
  Week 1 — map the territory @a:book
    [ ] Find the 3 best sources — one book, one course, one human
    [ ] 20 minutes a day, blocked in the calendar
    [ ] Write down ten dumb questions
  Week 2 — hands dirty @a:idea
    [ ] Copy the masters: reproduce three examples
    [ ] Break something, then fix it
    [ ] Share one tiny win with one person
  Week 3 — one real project @a:rocket
    [ ] Pick a project you actually need
    [ ] Ship the ugly version by Friday
    [ ] Ask one expert for brutal feedback
  Week 4 — ship and teach @a:trophy
    [ ] Polish it, publish it
    [ ] Teach it once: a post, or a lunch talk
    [ ] Decide: go deeper, or bank it
  The method @a:clock
    Same time every day beats motivation
    Recall beats rereading — test yourself
    Rest is part of the reps
```
