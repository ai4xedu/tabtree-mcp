## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
🔁 Habit builder
  🌅 Morning
    ☀️ Daylight in my eyes within twenty minutes @locked
      [x] Cue — feet on the floor
      [x] Two minutes outside is enough to count
      [ ] Thirty days without missing twice in a row
    💧 A full glass of water before the coffee @try
      [x] Glass left by the kettle the night before
      [ ] Fourteen days
    📓 Three lines in the notebook @try
      [ ] Notebook lives on the desk, open
  🌙 Evening
    📵 The phone charges outside the bedroom @locked
    📖 Ten pages before the light goes out @try
      [ ] Book already open on the pillow
    🛁 Lights down at 22:30 @design
      [ ] What would make it easy?
      [ ] What makes it hard tonight?
      [ ] What do I do instead of scrolling?
  💪 Body
    🚶 A walk after lunch, whatever the weather @locked
    🏋️ Two strength sessions a week @design
      [ ] Booked in the calendar, not "when I have time"
      [ ] A version that survives a bad week
    🧊 Thirty seconds of cold at the end of the shower @parked
  🧠 Focus
    🚫 No screens at the table @locked
    ⏱ One fifty-minute block before the inbox @design
      [ ] Which hour, exactly?
    📥 Inbox to zero on Friday, then closed @parked
  ✂️ Habits I am removing
    🍬 Nothing sweet after dinner @design
      [ ] Decide what replaces it, or it comes back
    📺 No autoplay — the next episode is a decision @parked
```
