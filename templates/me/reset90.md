## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
🔥 90-day reset
  🫀 Body
    😴 Sleep seven hours, fourteen nights in a row @doing
      [ ] The phone charges in another room
      [ ] Same wake-up time, weekends included
      [ ] One square on the calendar per night
    🏃 Move thirty minutes, four times a week @week
      [ ] Pick the sport I would still do on a bad day
      [ ] Book the first three sessions today
    🥗 Cook three real meals a week @later
    [x] 🚭 Drop the one thing I already know is hurting me @week
  🧠 Mind
    📵 Phone under two hours a day @doing
      [ ] Delete the two worst apps
      [ ] Screen goes grey after 21:00
      [ ] Charger stays downstairs
    📚 Finish the book I keep pretending I have read @week
    🧘 Ten quiet minutes before anyone else is awake @later
  💼 Work
    🚀 Ship the thing I keep postponing @doing
      [ ] The ugly version, in public
      [ ] A date, told to someone who will ask about it
      [ ] Cut the two features nobody asked for
    🙅 Say no to one thing that drains me @week
    💰 One honest conversation about money @later
  ❤️ People
    ☎️ Call the person I keep meaning to call @week
    🍽 One dinner a month, phones in a bowl @later
    [x] 🙏 Say the thank-you I never said @week
  🌱 Me
    ✍️ Three lines in a notebook before the light goes out @week
    🎨 One thing purely for pleasure, with no output @later
    🗺 Book the trip instead of talking about it @later
```
