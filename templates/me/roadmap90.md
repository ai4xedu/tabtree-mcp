## The outline

This is the exact outline TabTree builds. Indentation is the hierarchy, and
`[ ]` marks a checkbox. You can paste it straight into TabTree, or into any
outliner that understands indentation.

```text
Learn — skill or subject
  Days 1–10 · Orientation
    [ ] Map the whole field once, badly
    [ ] Find the three sources everyone cites
    [ ] List the words I am pretending to understand
    [ ] Build one tiny thing, end to end
  Days 11–30 · Fundamentals
    [ ] One book or course, actually finished
    [ ] Notes as a map, not as highlights
    [ ] Each week: build something that breaks
    [ ] First public post — including what I got wrong
  Days 31–60 · Real work
    [ ] A project someone other than me will use
    [ ] The part I keep avoiding — do that one first
    [ ] Find one person who is ahead of me
    [ ] Ship at day 45, even if it is ugly
  Days 61–80 · Depth
    [ ] Read the source, not the tutorial
    [ ] Rebuild from scratch the thing I copied
    [ ] Teach it to someone — where I stumble is the gap
  Days 81–90 · Proof
    [ ] The artefact that proves I can do it
    [ ] What I would tell someone starting today
    [ ] The next ninety days, decided now
```
